## Findings

All numbers are for product commit `bfbfa505`, Valkey 9.1.3, 6 nodes and a node timeout of 5 s. 390 trials were recorded, 30 per scenario. Section numbers point at the tables below.

1. **No control raised an event.** None of the 210 control trials raised an event at any coverage, any N, either monitor position or any join variant (section 6). Per scenario that is 0 of 30, with a 95% upper bound of 11% per trial; pooled over the seven different operations it is 0 of 210, upper bound 1.8%. The views of two nodes did differ during a clean join (29 of 30 trials), a legacy reshard (20 of 30) and an atomic migration (5 of 30), but never for longer than 4 s, 2 s and 1 s (section 2). On false positives N = 2, N = 3 and N = 5 are a tie: all three are zero.

2. **Both partitions were raised by every setting when the monitor could reach every node.** Zone partition and minority partition: 100% [89–100] at every coverage and every N (section 1). Coverage changed only the time (section 12). Against what ships today (zone 1, N = 3, median 18–19 s after the cut), k=1 was 3.8 s later in the minority partition and 5.3 s later in the zone partition, and k=2 was 0.9 s and 1.6 s later. k=3 and zone 2 were within 0.2 s. k=4, k=5 and all nodes were 1.1–1.5 s sooner in the minority partition and a tie in the zone partition.

3. **Each round of persistence cost 5 s and bought nothing measurable here.** With the same coverage, N = 2 raised 5.0 s sooner than N = 3 and N = 5 raised 10.0 s later, in every trial. Raise rates were the same in every trial (section 12). Events closed 8 s, 13 s and 23 s after the heal at N = 2, 3 and 5 (section 8).

4. **A monitor on one side of the cut raised nothing, at any coverage.** When the monitor can read only the nodes on the seed's side, both partitions gave 0% at every coverage, asking all nodes included (section 4): the nodes it can still read agree with each other. More coverage does not fix this. On the same recordings the seed's own `cluster_state` turned to fail for every cut-off seed (60 of 60) and for 64 of 120 (minority partition) and 9 of 120 (zone partition) seeds on the larger side, 7–8 s after the cut (section 9).

5. **Killed primary, paused node and flapping node were never raised, and no coverage could have raised them.** 0 of 30 at every setting (section 1). The nodes' views disagreed for at most 2 s, 2 s and 5 s in these scenarios, with medians of 1 s, 1 s and 3 s (section 2); an event at N = 2 needs a disagreement at two rounds 5 s apart. At a 5 s node timeout the cluster agrees that the node failed faster than the detector's round interval. This is not a coverage miss. A 15 s node timeout was not measured.

6. **Coverage mattered in one scenario: the failure-flag dispute of the partial join.** In all 30 trials every node listed the joiner within 1.9–4.9 s, and then the three nodes cut off from it kept it flagged as failed while the other three did not, for the rest of the 150 s window (141–145 s), ending within 1 s of the heal. Raise rates (section 1): k=1 57% [54–60], k=2 79% [72–84], k=3 90% [83–96], zone 1 89% [82–96], and 100% [89–100] for k=4, k=5, zone 2 and all nodes. zone 1 and k=3 are a tie. k=4, k=5, zone 2 and all nodes are 10.6 points [4.4, 17.8] above zone 1 and 3.0–5.8 s sooner (section 12). Counting only membership after 90 s raised nothing (section 10), because membership was never in dispute for that long.

7. **k=1 follows the accused.** It asks no second node until the seed's own view flags someone, so it costs no extra read while idle and about one extra read per round during a fault (section 11). That is why it is 4–5 s later on the partitions and misses 43% of the partial-join replays, and why it sees almost nothing from inside a cut (7% on the partial join, section 4).

8. **zone 2 is not full fan-out at 6 nodes.** 2 per zone gives primaries only when every zone has a primary. Here every zone has one, so zone 2 compared 3 views per round when the seed was a primary and 4 when it was a replica (3.5 on average), against 3 for zone 1 and 6 for all nodes (section 11). Asking all nodes is its own row: 5 extra reads and 3.7 KB per round, 60 extra reads a minute, against 2 extra reads and 1.5 KB for zone 1.

9. **The single-view signals are a different kind of signal, and one of them fired in a control.** The duplicate-primary signal fired in no scenario. The cluster-state signal fired in the rolling restart: each restarted primary reported its own state as fail for 2–3 s after it came back, in 30 of 30 trials (section 9). The divergence detector raised nothing there.

What Stage 1 does not settle: at 6 nodes zone 1 and k=3 pick nearly the same nodes, no control disagreed for as long as one round, and the three short faults never disagreed long enough for any setting to raise. So these recordings do not separate N = 2 from N = 5 on false positives, and they separate coverages only on the partial join. 12 and 30 nodes and a 15 s node timeout are not measured.

Recording quality (section 13): 12 attempts were dropped because host load crossed the limit and were recorded again; no kept trial had a p99 tick lateness over 15 ms; the shipped setting raised no event before the injection in any trial.
